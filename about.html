⚜ About the Chronicle ⚜

Gather around, travelers.

Within these pages lie tales of heroism, betrayal, questionable leadership decisions, suspicious dungeon activities, and enough gossip to keep the taverns busy for months.

This zine serves as a record of our kingdom's adventures, scandals, triumphs, and unforgettable moments. While every effort has been made to preserve the truth, some ravens may have embellished their reports after a few drinks.

Read at your own risk.
